## Direction

A calm engineering notebook: warm off-white, ink-colored text, blue accents, editorial sans-serif with selective italic serif, and small monospaced labels. Visual material explains the work: an end-to-end architecture, a proposal pipeline, scoped knowledge retrieval, and conceptual quantization diagrams.

The structure combines an engineering portfolio with a research homepage: introduction → selected work → publications → experience and mentorship → engineering toolkit → contact. Internal projects use expandable notes; public repositories have direct GitHub links. The complete content remains readable without JavaScript. Theme, Korean translation, filters, project-note dialogs, and citation copying progressively enhance it.
